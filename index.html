My Journey

Hear my story as an AI innovator, recorded at 03:07 AM CDT, October 01, 2025.

My journey began in Tirupati, India, where I earned a Diploma in Computer Engineering with a remarkable 93.6% at S.V Govt. Polytechnic. This foundation led me to JNTUA College of Engineering, where I pursued a B.Tech in Computer Science, graduating with an 8.1/10. Today, I’m a Master’s student at Texas A&M University-San Antonio, achieving a 3.87/4.0, diving deep into the world of autonomous AI systems.

From Chennai to San Antonio, I’ve shaped intelligent solutions. At Digitalsoft AI Solutions, I engineered GenAI chat assistants with LangChain, optimizing scalability with AWS. Now, as a Graduate Research Assistant at Texas A&M, I’m crafting cross-domain retrieval systems with CLIP, cutting latency by 20% on massive datasets, and enhancing medical imaging with probabilistic embeddings—work recognized at IEEE and AAAI.
